## STORY HIGHLIGHTS SETUP STEPS:

1. Instagram profile kholo
2. "+" ya "New" button se New Highlight banao
3. Pehle wo stories post karo jo highlight me dalgna hai
4. Story ke neeche "Highlight" button click karo
5. Highlight ka naam likho: "WEBSITES"
6. Cover image select karo: v2 dark navy wala
7. Done!

Har highlight ke liye repeat karo.
